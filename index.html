Our Chronicles

Heritage Legacy

1910

Founded by Shri Kanhaiya Lal Saraf

Establishing our primary foundations in the heritage capital of gold embroidery—Varanasi. Built on the core values of gold purity checkups and verified hallmark trading.

Founder

Mukund Lal & Balaram Das Saraf Era

The legacy was carried forward by Shri Mukund Lal Saraf and Shri Balaram Das Saraf, making Kanhaiya Lal Saraf Jewellers (KLSJ) a leading name in Purvanchal jewellery trading.

2006

Birth of Brand Trueso

Shri Abhay Agrawal Ji took a major step forward, establishing the modern brand name 'Trueso' to bring Varanasi its first absolute 100% Hallmarked Jewellery Showroom.

Today

100% Certified Showrooms

Recognized as the primary destination for 100% Hallmarked Jewellery across Eastern U.P., operating two flagships stores in Varanasi (Sigra & Godowlia).
